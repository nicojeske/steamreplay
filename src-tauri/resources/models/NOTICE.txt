SteamReplay includes mn10_as.onnx, the EfficientAT "mn10_as" audio tagging model by Florian Schmid
(https://github.com/fschmid56/EfficientAT), exported to ONNX without its mel front-end. It finds
laughter in the recordings' audio (M15).

The EfficientAT code and pre-trained weights are licensed under the MIT License; the full text is
in LICENSE-EfficientAT.txt next to this file. The weights were trained on AudioSet
(https://research.google.com/audioset/).
